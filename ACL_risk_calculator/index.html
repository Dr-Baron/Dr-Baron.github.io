ACL Injury Risk Calculator

Enter your scores to obtain your individual regression model based estimate of the ACL injury risk.

Training Intensity is your average intensity of training sessions on a scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high).

Fatigue Score is your subjective fatigue level on a scale of 1 (low) to 10 (high).

Load Balance Score is a calculated score (0–100) indicating the balance between training load and recovery. A higher score reflects a better balance.

The original scores shown are the estimated population medians. Replace them with your individual scores and press "Calculate". You can also choose a different confidence level.

Model prediction

Enter scores and select Calculate
Prediction uses the fitted linear regression model.

This tool is not a precise medical diagnosis, a validated clinical risk assessment, or a substitute for professional medical advice. Do not use it alone to make return-to-play decisions.
