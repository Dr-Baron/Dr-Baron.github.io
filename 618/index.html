Stat 618 Bayesian Statistics

Spring-2018, Wednesday, 5:30 - 8:00 pm
in DMTI-109 on the East Campus

The Bayesian Statistics course presents a practical introduction to the main principles and applications of modern statistical decision theory, with a special focus on Bayesian modeling, data analysis, inference, and optimal decision making. The course shows how three main sources of information – observed data, anticipated consequences of our decisions in the form of a loss function or risk, and knowledge before the experiment in the form of a prior distribution – are combined to produce optimal statistical solutions. Elaborated Bayesian methodology will be applied to real problems using statistical software, R and BUGS.
